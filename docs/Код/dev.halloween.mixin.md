# dev.halloween.mixin

Файл — строк — о чём (первая фраза комментария класса). Обновляется tools/obsidian_export.pl.

- **BlockStateBaseMixin** (`src/main/java/dev/halloween/mixin/BlockStateBaseMixin.java`, 32) — Doors have no collision for the Shape while he is in Shape Jump.
- **ItemEntityMixin** (`src/main/java/dev/halloween/mixin/ItemEntityMixin.java`, 22) — Michael carries his knife and nothing else: he walks over what lies about without picking it up.
- **LivingEntitySprintMixin** (`src/main/java/dev/halloween/mixin/LivingEntitySprintMixin.java`, 35) — Survivors run a little faster (6 m/s), and not at all while out of breath (see SprintRules, Stamina).
- **SnowballMixin** (`src/main/java/dev/halloween/mixin/SnowballMixin.java`, 21) — Bricks, rocks, flares and the like fly as snowballs carrying their item; where they land is ours to decide.
